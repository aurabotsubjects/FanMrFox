## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Each numbered item below is a **complete, ready-to-paste prompt** — copy the whole quoted sentence into your image generator, no editing required. Every prompt already has the visual identity and style guardrails baked in, so they'll come out consistent with each other even if you generate them on different days or in different tools.

**Visual identity for this app — "autumn wood, night sky and underground tunnels":** deep night-sky navy and indigo, glowing sunset orange and gold, fox-russet orange, autumn-leaf red, oak-leaf olive green and warm earth brown, with a recurring motif of acorns, oak leaves, paw prints and arched tunnel doorways glowing with warm lantern light. This matches your classroom poster (the sunset hillside with the fox lookout) and the app's `styles.css` palette, so generated images will sit naturally alongside the buttons, cards and headings.

**Style reference:** the "Coming Soon" poster you made — rich painterly children's-book illustration, warm glowing light, autumn leaves framing the edges. Add "in the same painterly autumn style as the poster" if your generator lets you upload the poster as a reference.

⚠️ **Copyright note — read before generating anything:** Do not ask an image generator to recreate Quentin Blake's illustrations from the book, the cover art of any edition, or stills/character designs from the 2009 film. Every prompt below is written to produce **original scenery, symbols and silhouettes** inspired by the story's settings and themes. Where an animal appears it is a generic silhouette or paw-print, never a copy of a specific illustrated character. If a generator produces something that looks like the book's or the film's artwork, regenerate with more emphasis on "original, generic, not a specific character design".

**Where to put the finished files:** save each image into `mrfox/images/web/` using the **exact filename** shown beside each prompt (as `.webp`). The site already contains simple placeholder images with these names, so just overwrite them one at a time — the app will pick them up automatically. (Convert PNG → WebP with any free converter, or keep the PNG and change the extension in the code if you prefer.)

**Recommended image sizes:**
- Hero banner / weekly banners: 1600×600px (wide, landscape)
- Icons: 512×512px (square, transparent background where possible)
- Certificate border: 1600×1200px (landscape)
- Badges: 512×512px (square)
- Tunnel-feast background: 1600×900px

**Common style line (already inside every prompt, shown here so you can tweak it once):**
`flat-to-painterly children's-book illustration, warm glowing light, night-sky navy / sunset orange and gold / fox-russet / autumn-leaf red / olive green palette, no text, original imagery only`
